ChatPlus Desktop for macOS

A native Mac app for Synology ChatPlus and Synology Chat. Sign in with just your QuickConnect ID; no server addresses to figure out.

Download for Mac

Beta · Apple Silicon and Intel · macOS 11 or later · All versions

Install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag ChatPlus Desktop into Applications.
  2. Open it. Because this beta isn’t signed by Apple, macOS will say it can’t verify the developer. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to ChatPlus Desktop. You only need to do this once.

Comfortable with Terminal? This does the same as step 3:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/ChatPlus Desktop.app"

Set up

  1. Choose Synology ChatPlus (or Synology Chat).
  2. Enter your QuickConnect ID, for example mynas. You can also enter your NAS address, such as https://nas.example.com:5001.
  3. Sign in with your DSM account and tick Stay signed in.

On a laptop, the app finds your NAS again by itself when you move between home and the office.

From version 0.7.1, if the app can’t reach your NAS it tells you why (address not found, no answer, untrusted certificate) instead of showing an empty window. Click Edit connection to fix the address, or Try again once the NAS is awake.

Good to know

  • This is a beta for testing. If something breaks, please report an issue.
  • Because the beta isn’t signed, desktop notifications appear under “Script Editor”, and macOS may ask once to allow them.
  • Moving between your home network and the internet can ask you to sign in again.
  • No ads and no tracking. Your messages and password never pass through this app’s developers.
